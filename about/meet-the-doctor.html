Un dentista centrado en el paciente

Dra. Dubois is deeply passionate about making dentistry accessible, stress-free, and empowering. She sees dental care not just as a clinical service, but as a path to overall health, confidence, and well-being. Her approach blends technical excellence with emotional intelligence — and her patients appreciate both.

She specializes in creating personalized treatment plans that reflect your individual goals, lifestyle, and comfort level. Whether you're coming in for routine care or a complete smile transformation, she’ll walk with you every step of the way — no judgment, no pressure.

Formación y Educación

Dra. Dubois’s path to dentistry began in her hometown of Grosse Pointe, Michigan, but her curiosity and sense of adventure took her to the East Coast, where she earned her undergraduate degree from Boston University. After exploring city life in Boston, she returned to Michigan to pursue her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) at the University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry.

Always committed to growth, she continues to expand her expertise through advanced training, hands-on workshops, and continuing education courses. Her goal is to bring the latest techniques and highest standard of care to her patients — always.
